About

Italian tattooer guesting across Europe: Milano, London, Berlin, Zurich. Currently based in Milano.

As a child I started drawing from life, and figurative art has stayed with me ever since. I fell in love with tattooing during my first years of artistic training, and I have been tattooing since 2013, moving through different styles before finding my own.

My work is blackwork. I build dramatic, sharp subjects and concepts through high contrast and stippling: dark slender creatures, weapons, forms taken from medieval imagery and from organic, bio-inspired shapes.

Most of my work is freehand. I draw directly on the skin, following the lines and movement of the body, so every piece grows with its shape and belongs only to the person who wears it.

But I still love working on paper. Drawing is where the idea takes shape, and the skin is where it comes to life. That is why I am always up for custom projects, built from scratch around your idea.

I keep moving, travelling to make art, meet other artists and keep growing, wherever the work takes me.

How to book

  1. Write to me via DM, WhatsApp or email: introduce yourself, give a brief description of your idea and attach references if possible, for good communication between us.
  2. To proceed I need a deposit, which counts as part of the payment. It is the only way to book and start working on your project.
  3. If you don't show up on the day of your appointment you will lose your deposit. You have up to 24 hours before the session to let me know and change the date or any details (serious or exceptional cases excepted).

Deposits are non-refundable
